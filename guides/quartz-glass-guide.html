Grounded 2 Quartz Glass: How to Get Phantom Quartz & Craft It

Updated August 22, 2026 - Into the Abyss update

Quartz Glass is one of the most important new materials in Grounded 2's Into the Abyss update — it's required for the Diving Suit, the Kiln, and several late-game recipes. But you can't just pick it up: it's a processed material with a full crafting chain. This guide walks you through every step.

The short version
Mine Phantom Quartz in the crystal cave → grind it into Crushed Quartz at a Quern → craft Fire Brick at an Oven → scan the Fire Brick to unlock the Kiln → craft Quartz Glass at the Kiln.

Step 1: Find the Phantom Quartz Crystal Cave

Phantom Quartz is a new crystal added with the Into the Abyss update. It only spawns in one specific location:

  • Travel to the east side of the Gnarly Bark tree — the huge tree area of the map.
  • Find the spot where the pond meets the tree trunk.
  • Enter the underground cave there. Bring a torch — it's dark inside.
  • Inside you'll find an underground river and rocky terrain. The glowing crystal formations on the rocks and in the river are Phantom Quartz.

Tip: Mine as much as you can carry in one trip. The full Diving Suit + Kiln crafting chain needs a large amount, and you don't want to repeat the trip. Interact with the crystal formations directly to harvest.

Step 2: Build a Quern and Make Crushed Quartz

The Quern is the grinding station that turns raw Phantom Quartz into usable Crushed Quartz.

Quern Materials

Build it from the Utilities tab of your building menu. Once built, interact with the Quern and select Crushed Quartz to start grinding your Phantom Quartz.

Don't skip this
After crafting any new item, scan it at a Resource Analyzer (or Ranger Outpost scanner). Scanning unlocks new recipes — this is how the recipe chain progresses.

Step 3: Craft Fire Bricks at the Oven

Fire Brick is the gateway item that unlocks the Kiln blueprint.

Fire Brick recipe (at your Oven):

  • 1x Crushed Quartz
  • 4x Clay

Craft one Fire Brick, then immediately scan it at the Ranger Outpost. Scanning the Fire Brick unlocks several recipes — including the one for Quartz Glass and the Kiln blueprint.

Step 4: Build the Kiln

Build the Kiln from the Utilities blueprint menu once you've scanned the Fire Brick.

Step 5: Craft Quartz Glass

Interact with your Kiln and select Quartz Glass:

Quartz Glass recipe:

  • 2x Crushed Quartz
  • 1x Horsetail Stem

That's it — you now have Quartz Glass. Stock up on Horsetail Stems before big crafting sessions (10–20 stems covers the full Diving Suit chain). Horsetails look the same dead or alive but turn brown when dry — dead ones can be picked up without tools, live ones need a Tier 2 snipper or axe to cut underwater.

FAQ

Can I buy Quartz Glass anywhere?

No — it's craft-only. The full chain (mine → Quern → Oven → Kiln) is mandatory.

My Quern/Kiln blueprint isn't showing up

You likely haven't scanned the prerequisite item. Scan Crushed Quartz and Fire Brick at a Ranger Outpost to progress the recipe unlocks.

How much Phantom Quartz should I mine?

At minimum, enough for: 8 Fire Bricks (Kiln) + 5 Quartz Glass (Diving Suit) + extras for upgrades. Fill your inventory — you'll thank yourself later.
