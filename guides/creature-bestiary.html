Grounded 2 Creature Bestiary

Updated August 15, 2026 - 30+ creatures including The Toxic Tangle additions

Every creature in Grounded 2, organized by threat level. Use PEEP.R in-game to confirm weaknesses before engaging. Damage types: Slashing, Piercing, Blunt, Spicy, Fresh, Sour, Venom, and Sonic.

How to read this bestiary
Weakness = use this damage type for bonus damage. Resist = reduced damage, avoid. Drops = materials you can harvest from the corpse.

Bosses

Snake Colossus

Weakness: Slashing Resist: Piercing Boss Drops: Serpent Hide, Colossus Scale

You don't fight this creature, you ride it. All four scales of the Snake Colossus can be mounted. Its bite deals 1,000 damage that cannot be blocked. Serpent armor set crafted from its drops offers the highest defense in the game.

Tarantula (Boss)

Weakness: Sour Resist: Slashing Boss Drops: Tarantula Web, Venom Gland

The tarantula boss guards the deep arena. Bring a Sour Banger or Sour Katanga and plenty of healing. The arena has limited cover, so perfect blocking is essential. Recommend Tier 2+ armor before attempting.

The Masked Stranger

Weakness: Varies Recurring encounter

Not a creature you fight for loot, but a recurring story NPC. She retreats after your first encounter but returns with allies. Track her through the Garden biome quests in The Toxic Tangle update.

High-Threat Creatures

Orb Weaver Spider

Weakness: Blunt Resist: Piercing Drops: Spider Web, Spider Fang

Patrols webbed areas. Its web shot immobilizes you, making you vulnerable to follow-up attacks. Use a Warhammerlet or Caustic Ruin for blunt damage. Burn webs with Spicy arrows to clear escape routes.

Wasp

Weakness: Fresh Resist: Spicy Drops: Wasp Part, Wasp Wing New (Toxic Tangle)

Fast-flying attacker that strikes from above. Great Mint Arrows are your best counter. The Wasp Helm and Wasp Breastplate set boosts ranged damage.

Wasp Drone

Weakness: Sonic Resist: Slashing Drops: Drone Core, Wasp Part New (Toxic Tangle)

Smaller, more numerous than standard Wasps. Swarm in groups. Cricket Lute Bow's Sonic arrows are devastating against them. Area-of-effect weapons like the Sour Banger also work well.

Rust Beetle

Weakness: Sour Resist: Slashing, Blunt Drops: Rust, Rust Beetle Shell New (Toxic Tangle)

Heavily armored. Standard weapons bounce off its shell. Only Sour-element weapons deal full damage. Sour Banger is the ideal counter. Some Rust Beetles are covered in Tang, making them more aggressive.

Medium-Threat Creatures

Potato Beetle

Weakness: Piercing Resist: Blunt Drops: Potato Beetle Shell, Potato New (Toxic Tangle)

Bulky and slow but hits hard. Circle-strafe to avoid its charge attack. Black Ant Sword and Discord are effective. Potato Beetle armor set provides high defense at moderate crafting cost.

Earwig (Pincher)

Weakness: Slashing Resist: Piercing Drops: Earwig Part, Chrysalis Hide New (Toxic Tangle)

Coordinated pack hunters. Two variants: Pincher (melee) and Whipper (ranged pincer throw). They communicate and flank. Engage one at a time by retreating to chokepoints. Earwig Sword is crafted from their parts.

Earwig (Whipper)

Weakness: Slashing Resist: Piercing Drops: Earwig Part, Chrysalis Hide New (Toxic Tangle)

Ranged variant that throws its pincers like projectiles. Close the distance quickly. If fighting a mixed group, take out Whippers first so you can focus on Pinchers without dodging projectiles.

Woolly Aphid

Weakness: Spicy Resist: Fresh Drops: Woolly Aphid Fuzz, Aphid Honey New (Toxic Tangle)

Soon-to-be fan favorite. Fluffy, deceptively tanky, and emits a slowing aura. Great Spicy Arrows melt through its fluff. Aphid Honey is a valuable food item that restores health over time.

Cricket

Weakness: Sonic Resist: none Drops: Cricket Part, Chrysalis Hide New (Toxic Tangle)

Hops unpredictably, making melee difficult. Cricket Lute Bow's Sonic arrows are the ideal counter. Cricket armor set boosts mutation cooldown speed.

Low-Threat Creatures

Mite

Weakness: All Drops: Mite Part, Web Fiber

Your training dummy. Low damage, low health, spawns in groups near grassy areas. Any weapon works. Good for practicing perfect blocks.

Larva

Weakness: Blunt Drops: Larva Spike, Grub Hide

Slow and predictable. Warhammerlet one-shots them. Grub Hide from larvae is needed for the Canteen recipe.

Black Worker Ant

Weakness: Slashing Resist: Piercing Drops: Black Ant Part, Ant Mandible

Non-aggressive unless you attack first or approach the colony. Good early-game material source. Use Black Ant Sword for irony and efficiency.

Black Soldier Ant

Weakness: Sour Resist: Slashing Drops: Black Ant Part, Ant Mandible, Ant Egg

Aggressive defenders. Guard the ant hill where you find Buggy eggs. Sour Banger works best. Don't fight more than 2 at once without a buggy mount.

Firefly

Weakness: Fresh Drops: Firefly Head Lamp, Firefly Part

Mostly harmless and nocturnal. The Firefly Head Lamp is a valuable crafting drop that provides hands-free light. Hunt them at night near the pond area.

Mosquito

Weakness: Blunt Drops: Mosquito Beak, Mosquito Head

Annoying but weak. They drain health on hit, healing themselves. A quick Warhammerlet swing ends them. No longer falls asleep mid-air (bug fix in v0.3).

Combat Strategy Summary

SituationRecommended approach
Single tough enemyPEEP.R first, match weakness, perfect block its attacks
Group of enemiesRetreat to chokepoint, use AoE weapons (Sour Banger), thin the herd
Flying enemiesBow with matched element arrows, or Cricket Lute Bow for Sonic weakness
Armored enemiesUse Sour-element weapons to bypass armor
Boss fightsFull armor set, shield, 2 weapon types, healing items, learn attack patterns
