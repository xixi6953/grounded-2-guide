Grounded 2 Buggy Mounts Guide

Updated August 15, 2026 - All mounts including Ladybug Buggy (Toxic Tangle)

Buggy mounts are the biggest game-changer in Grounded 2. They transform how you explore, fight, and carry loot. This guide covers every buggy type, how to get the eggs, hatchery recipes, upgrade paths, and mounted abilities.

When to get your first buggy
Aim for hours 5-7 of gameplay. The story quests naturally lead you to this. You need a Hatchery (unlocks through the quest line) and a buggy egg.

How to Get Each Egg

Red Soldier Ant Egg

Found inside ant hills. The main ant hill is in the Grasslands biome. Enter cautiously: Soldier Ants are aggressive and spawn in groups. Bring a Sour Banger or Sour Katanga (they're weak to Sour damage) and clear the entrance before going deep. The eggs are in clusters at the bottom of the hill.

Orb Weaver Egg

Common mistake
The Orb Weaver egg is NOT inside the Spider Den. It's on a web in the trees ABOVE the Spider Den. Don't go in looking for it.

Climb the grass stems near the Spider Den area and look up. The egg sits on a web platform suspended in the trees. Reach it by climbing the stem structures and jumping across. Bring a Pebblet Spear for the occasional web-spiders on the path.

Ladybug Egg (Toxic Tangle)

Found at the Ladybug Nest in the new Garden biome. The Garden is accessible after completing the first act of the main story or by following the Toxic Tangle questline. The nest is in the pumpkin patch area of the greenhouse. Ladybug Buggies are heavy-duty and tank-style, making them the best combat mount currently in the game.

Minotaur Mount

Available through the Founder's Pack content or a specific quest in the Garden biome. More of a novelty mount, but its ground slam AoE is useful against swarms of small creatures.

Buggy Abilities

Red Soldier Ant Buggy

  • Charge - sprint into enemies to knock them back and deal damage
  • Ant Recruitment - nearby wild ants may join you in combat
  • Extra Inventory - doubles your carry capacity

Best for: Early-mid game exploration and material runs. The recruitment ability makes ant hill runs much safer.

Orb Weaver Buggy

  • Web Shot - fires web that immobilizes enemies and creatures
  • Wall Climb - can climb vertical surfaces (grass stems, rock faces)
  • Extra Inventory - larger than Red Ant Buggy

Best for: Exploration and reaching hard-to-access areas. The web shot is excellent for crowd control. Climbing ability opens shortcuts across the map.

Ladybug Buggy (Toxic Tangle)

  • Water Cannon - pressurized blast that knocks airborne pests out of the sky and rinses away Sour Tang buildup
  • Ram Charge - powerful headbutt that deals heavy damage and stuns
  • HP Regeneration - slowly heals over time, making it the most durable mount
  • Extra Inventory - largest storage of all buggies

Best for: Combat and the Garden biome. The water cannon is the only reliable counter to airborne enemies like Wasps and Wasp Drones. HP regen means you rarely need to dismount mid-fight.

Garden access via Ladybug Buggy
The Ladybug Buggy's water cannon is needed to clear Tang buildup blocking certain greenhouse entrances. If you're stuck entering parts of the Garden biome, prioritize getting this mount.

Buggy gate
Six Mega Molars are gated behind buggy access. You need a mount to reach them. This creates a natural progression: get your first buggy, access new molars, upgrade the buggy, access more.

Buggy Tips

  • Whistle to call - your buggy comes when you whistle. If it's stuck, it will pathfind to you.
  • Don't share buggies - if two players whistle at the same time, a client may accidentally take over the host's buggy (known bug, being worked on).
  • Don't ride off structures - being knocked off your Orb Weaver Buggy can cause instant death in some edge cases.
  • Hauling Hero doesn't work on buggies - the Hauling Hero mutation only applies on foot, not while mounted.
  • Omni-Tool from buggy - chopping resources while standing on a built structure is easier now (v0.3 fix). You can harvest from your buggy's back.

Which Buggy Should You Get First?

Red Soldier Ant Buggy is the easiest to get early. The ant hill is accessible from the start, and the egg recipe is simple. Use it until you can reach the Orb Weaver egg or the Garden biome for the Ladybug Buggy.

If you're following the story, the quest line naturally directs you to the Red Soldier Ant first. Don't skip ahead unless you're confident in combat.
