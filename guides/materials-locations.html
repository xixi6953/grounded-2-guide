Grounded 2 Materials & Locations Guide

Updated August 15, 2026 - All biomes including The Toxic Tangle Garden

Every crafting material in Grounded 2 and where to find it. Organized by biome. Each entry includes the Omni-Tool tier required to harvest it (Tier 1 = starting axe, Tier 2 = upgraded, Tier 3 = late game).

Pro tip
Always carry a Canteen and Chests on expeditions. Your pockets fill up fast. Drop a chest at a landmark to cache materials for later pickup with your buggy.

The pond area was partially locked before the August 11 update. If you're on an older save, the Into the Abyss content should auto-unlock. If not, complete the first act of the main story.

Milk Molar checklist
There are 46 Regular Milk Molars and 22 Mega Milk Molars. Regular ones need a Tier 1 shovel. Mega ones need Tier 2. Six are gated behind buggy access. Use the map filter to track which ones you're missing.
