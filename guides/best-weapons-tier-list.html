Grounded 2 Best Weapons Tier List

Updated August 15, 2026 - Includes The Toxic Tangle update (v0.3) weapons

Every weapon in Grounded 2 ranked from S tier (must-craft) to C tier (situational). This list includes all weapons from launch through The Toxic Tangle update. Crafting costs are sourced from the workbench UI.

How to use this list
Tiers reflect overall effectiveness across the game: damage output, versatility, upgrade potential, and material cost efficiency. A lower-tier weapon may still be the best choice against a specific creature with the right damage type.

Weapon Upgrade Path

Weapons can be upgraded at the Smithing Station using Jewel and Raw Science. Each upgrade tier increases base damage and adds a perk:

  • Level 1 - +3 damage, unlocks first perk slot
  • Level 2 - +6 damage total, unlocks second perk slot
  • Level 3 - +10 damage total, final perk

Perks are randomized from a pool. Use PEEP.R on your target creature to match your weapon's damage type to their weakness.

Pro tip
Always carry two weapon types with different damage elements. Slashing + Sour and Piercing + Venom covers most creature weaknesses in the game.

Shield Recommendation

Don't forget your shield. The Black Ant Shield remains the best all-round shield, with high block reduction and a chance to reflect damage on perfect blocks. Craft it from 2x Black Ant Part, 3x Berry Leather, and 2x Sap.
