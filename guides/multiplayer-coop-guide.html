Grounded 2 Multiplayer: Crossplay, Co-op & Shared Worlds Explained

Updated August 22, 2026

Grounded 2 is best played with friends — a backyard full of giant bugs is much friendlier when someone's watching your back. This guide covers everything about multiplayer: crossplay, player limits, the difference between Standard and Shared Worlds, and the cross-save details nobody tells you about.

Crossplay: Who Can Play With Whom

Grounded 2 supports full crossplay across all platforms:

  • Xbox Series X|S
  • PC via Steam
  • PC via Microsoft Store / Game Pass
  • PS5 (added with the August 2026 PS5 launch)

Any player on any platform can join any world. Crossplay is enabled by default — no settings to fiddle with.

The Cross-Save Catch (Steam Players Read This)

Cross-save is where it gets tricky. Your save data lives on Xbox/Microsoft's ecosystem by default:

  • Microsoft Store / Game Pass / Xbox: saves sync automatically through your Microsoft account. Switch between Xbox and PC freely.
  • Steam: the Steam version connects to your Microsoft account for multiplayer, but save syncing between Steam and other platforms has limitations — check the in-game prompt when linking accounts, and back up your saves before switching platforms.
  • PS5: PlayStation players link a Microsoft account for crossplay purposes.

Standard Worlds vs Shared Worlds

Grounded 2 offers two world types, and picking correctly saves headaches later:

Standard World (Host-Based)

  • The world lives on the host's machine. Friends join your game session.
  • If the host is offline, nobody can play in that world.
  • Best for: playing together with a fixed group, one person "owns" the progress.

Shared World (Always Available)

  • The world is stored in the cloud and shared with up to a small group of invited players.
  • Anyone in the group can play anytime, even if the original creator is offline.
  • Best for: friend groups in different time zones, or couples/siblings who share one world.
Which should you pick?
If you and your friends play at different times — or you don't want your base's progress frozen when you're away — choose Shared World. It's the single most recommended setting for co-op groups.

How to Invite Friends

  1. Load into your world.
  2. Open the pause menu and select the multiplayer/invite option (or use the console's system invite — Xbox Guide button / PS5 invite card).
  3. Your friend accepts, loads in next to you, and spawns with a basic starting kit.

What New Players Joining Your World Should Know

  • New joiners start from scratch gear-wise — hand them spare weapons and armor if you have extras.
  • Progress (quests, unlocked areas) is shared with the host's world state.
  • All players keep their own inventories between sessions in that world.

Dedicated Servers

As of the current Early Access build, Grounded 2 does not have user-hostable dedicated servers like some survival games. Shared Worlds are the "always on" solution. Watch patch notes — Obsidian has been adding server-side features with major updates like Into the Abyss.

Co-op Survival Tips

  • Split roles: one builder on the base, one farmer on materials, one hunter on food — progress multiplies.
  • Share mutations: check the mutations guide and diversify — one player on tank mutations, another on support.
  • Buggy mounts change co-op: mounted players can cover the map fast. See the Buggy mounts guide for egg locations and hatchery recipes.
  • Bed respawn points: each player sets their own — spread them across biomes for fast travel coverage.

FAQ

Can PS5 players play with Xbox players?

Yes. Since the PS5 launch, full crossplay includes PlayStation 5.

Can I transfer my solo world to a Shared World?

World conversion options depend on the current build — check the world settings menu. When in doubt, start your co-op world as Shared from day one.

Is there PvP?

No — Grounded 2 is purely co-op. You can hurt each other with friendly fire accidents, but there's no PvP mode.
