Grounded 2 Diving Suit: All 3 Aquatic Chips & How to Enter the Abyss

Updated August 22, 2026 - Into the Abyss update

The Abyss is Grounded 2's massive underwater biome added in the Into the Abyss update (August 11, 2026) — and the only way in is a full three-piece Diving Suit. This guide covers every step: the three Aquatic Turbo Discs, all crafting materials, and the exact route into the depths.

The one rule
The moon pool scanner at the Abyss entrance only opens when you wear all three pieces at once: Diving Helmet + Diving Suit Chestplate + Diving Boots. No single piece will pass the scan — no suit, no Abyss.

All 3 Aquatic Turbo Disc Locations

1. Gnarly Bark Disc — Unlocks Diving Boots

Location: Top of the Gnarly Bark tree.

  • Start near the memorial and head left. An Orb Weaver Buggy makes the climb dramatically easier — bring one to the base of the tree.
  • Ride up the mushrooms until you reach a cave entrance at the top. Your buggy can't enter, so continue on foot.
  • Move deeper into the cave, take a right, and climb the mushrooms higher. You can run past most enemies — don't waste time fighting.
  • At the top, take a left and follow the mushrooms along the outside of the tree. A small cave opening holds a dead body with the disc.

Unlock cost: 1,500 Raw Science at a Ranger Station.

2. Bridge Disc — Unlocks Diving Suit Chestplate

Location: The Bridge, on the right side of Skunk Pond.

  • Climb up by following the planks on the south side of the bridge.
  • At the top you'll meet Mess Officer Funk, a souped-up shield bug that behaves like a stink bug. Bring the Mantis Helmet (best gas mask) and Gastro Goo if the Woozy meter climbs.
  • You do not have to fight. The disc is on the ground next to a corpse — grab it and run. You can also cheese the fight by baiting him to jump between the bridge planks.

Unlock cost: 1,500 Raw Science.

3. Diving Station Disc — Unlocks Diving Helmet

Location: The Diving Station, just south of the "Skunk Pond" text on your map.

  • A Toe Biter Buggy and a Gill Tube are both strongly recommended — this trip involves water and cold zones.
  • Watch out for the massive Toe Biter guarding the area and navigate around it.
  • Reach the ladder entrance and drop into the small lab below. Go straight ahead — the disc is on the bench. There's a Ranger Station right behind the area.

Unlock cost: 1,500 Raw Science.

Build the Quern and Kiln First

Quartz Glass and Fire Brick are new processed materials — you cannot craft them until you set up two production stations. Both draw from a shared Phantom Quartz supply, so build these before you start gathering.

Quern (grinds Phantom Quartz into Crushed Quartz)

Materials: Clay x4, Sturdy Quartzite Shard x3, Lint Rope x2, Pinecone Piece x3, Striped Scorpion Chunk x2

Striped Scorpion Chunks are a new drop found in the Gnarly Bark area.

Kiln (crafts Quartz Glass)

Materials: Fire Brick x8, EverChar Coal Chunk x5, Green Shield Bug Parts x3, Rust x4, Horsetail Stem x3

Recipe: 2x Crushed Quartz + 1x Horsetail Stem = 1x Quartz Glass

Horsetails can be cut underwater or picked up dead on land (they turn brown when dry). You need a Tier 2 snipper or Tier 2 axe. Gather 10–20 stems to cover the full crafting chain.

Where to Mine Phantom Quartz

Go to the east side of the Gnarly Bark tree, where the pond meets the trunk, and enter the underground cave. The crystal formation inside is Phantom Quartz. Mine as much as you can carry — the full gear set needs a large amount. Bring a torch.

Entering the Abyss

  1. Craft all three Diving Suit pieces at a Workbench.
  2. Return to the Diving Station south of Skunk Pond.
  3. Take the transit system down.
  4. Walk through the moon pool scanner wearing all three pieces — the gate opens only for a complete suit.
  5. Welcome to the Abyss. Bring healing, light sources, and cold protection — the depths are unforgiving.
Before you dive
The Abyss is endgame content. Bring your best armor, upgraded weapons, and plenty of healing items. A Toe Biter Buggy helps enormously for reaching the entrance.

FAQ

Can I enter the Abyss without the full Diving Suit?

No. The moon pool scanner checks for all three equipped pieces. Two out of three fails the scan.

Do I need 4,500 Raw Science before starting?

You need it to unlock all three recipes at Ranger Stations. Grab all three discs first, then unlock the recipes — don't waste Raw Science on partial unlocks if you're short. Complete challenges and scan new items to earn more (see our materials guide for Raw Science farming tips).

Is the Diving Suit good outside the Abyss?

It's primarily a key item for Abyss access. For general water exploration, a Gill Tube and Toe Biter Buggy cover most needs — but the suit's cold resistance helps in deep water zones.
