Grounded 2 Best Armor: All 14 Sets Ranked

Updated August 15, 2026 - Covers Into the Abyss & Toxic Tangle content

Armor in Grounded 2 is about way more than defense. Every three-piece set unlocks a full-set bonus that can define your entire playstyle — from blocking tanks to charged bow snipers to staff-spamming wizards. This guide ranks all 14 sets and tells you exactly which one to craft for your build.

How set bonuses work
Equip all three pieces (head, chest, legs) of the same set to activate its passive skill. Mixing pieces means losing the bonus — so commit to a full set, and carry hazard gear as a swap.

S-Tier: The Build-Defining Sets

Snail Shell Armor — Best for Blocking Builds

Full-set bonus "Retaliation": blocked hits generate stacks (up to 5) that empower your next charged melee attack with a shockwave.

If you like the sword-and-shield loop, this is your endgame set. Every perfect block charges a massive counterattack. Pair it with a heavy weapon like the upgraded club line and stun-focused trinkets, and boss fights become a rhythm game: block, block, block, delete.

Crafting: farm Snail Shells from snails near damp zones; scan at a Resource Analyzer to unlock recipes.

Bee Armor — Best for Bow Builds

Full-set bonus "Apex Pollinator": perfectly charged bow shots release pollen that slows targets.

Bow users live and die by the charged shot. Bee armor rewards perfect timing with crowd control, letting you kite even fast predators like wolf spiders. Combine with the Marksman's headpiece stat line and tier-S bow options for the strongest ranged build in the game.

Crafting: hunt bees around flower zones in Brookhollow Park.

Sizzling Armor — Best for Staff Builds

Full-set bonus "Whizzy Wizard": staff hits increase staff attack speed, stacking up to 5 times.

Mage builds scale hard with attack speed. At five stacks you're machine-gunning elemental bolts. Bring chill/staff damage mutations from our mutations guide to complete the build.

Crafting: sizzle-themed materials from hot zones — carry water or Fuzzy pieces for the farming trips.

A-Tier: Excellent All-Rounders

Ladybug Armor — Best Solo Melee Set

Full-set bonus "Scarlet Embrace": restores a small amount of health when you block attacks.

The cleanest bridge from early to mid game. Sustain through blocking means fewer emergency retreats. If combat feels overwhelming, craft this first — see our beginner guide for the full early progression path.

Roach Armor — Best Survival Set

Full-set bonus "Adaptive Resistance": grants resistance to Venom, Chill, or Sizzle, stacking up to 5 times.

Ideal for exploring unknown zones and status-heavy encounters. Not the highest damage output, but it keeps you alive in the Community Garden's hazard-dense routes added in the Toxic Tangle update.

Tarantula & King Dozer Sets — Boss Reward Armor

Two of the strongest armor tracks come from optional bosses. The Tarantula set unlocks through the AXL encounter, and King Dozer gear is crafted from King Dozer scales. Both require serious preparation — see our boss guide for strategies.

C-Tier: Early Game Stepping Stones

Acorn Armor (Hardy Defense — damage resistance while blocking) is your first real tank set. Clover and Grub sets are stopgaps until you can farm bug-part sets. Craft them, use them, replace them — don't sink upgrade materials into tier-C gear.

Environmental & Utility Armor

These don't top the tier list but belong in your inventory as swap gear:

  • Fuzzy Armor — reduces Chill buildup; required for Ice Cream Cart and cold routes
  • Sizzling pieces — Sizzle resistance for hot zones
  • Gas Mask — Gas Guard; mandatory for gas-blocked passages
  • Roach pieces — restore health while looting corpses
Pro tip: carry a swap set
Hazard armor is a tool, not an outfit. Once the environment stops applying chill, sizzle, or gas, swap back to your combat set. Check the equipment panel after every swap to confirm your set skill is still active.

How Armor Crafting Works

  1. Find the core resource — hunt the relevant creature or gather the environmental material
  2. Scan it at a Resource Analyzer — unlocks the recipes (some need extra Brainpower levels)
  3. Craft all three pieces at a Workbench — the set bonus requires the complete outfit
  4. Upgrade at a Smithing Station — invest materials only in sets you'll keep using

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best armor in Grounded 2?

There's no single best — it depends on your build. Snail Shell for blocking melee, Bee for bows, Sizzling for staffs, Roach for general survival. Tarantula and King Dozer sets have the highest raw stats but come from optional bosses.

Can you mix armor pieces?

Yes, but you lose the three-piece set bonus. Only mix when a single utility perk (like the Gas Mask or a Roach piece) solves a specific problem.

What armor should I craft first?

Acorn Armor is the easiest early set. Progress to Ladybug by mid game, then specialize based on your weapon of choice.
