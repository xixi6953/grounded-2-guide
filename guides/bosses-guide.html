Grounded 2 All Bosses: How to Beat Every Boss

Updated August 15, 2026 - Covers Into the Abyss & Toxic Tangle content

Grounded 2's bosses are the ultimate gear check. Each one punishes unprepared players and rewards those who bring the right damage type, armor set, and strategy. This guide covers every boss in Brookhollow Park, including the story encounters and the deadly optional ones.

Before fighting any boss
Build a Lean-To near the arena and set it as your respawn. Bring a full canteen, cooked food, and a repair kit. Dying to a boss without a respawn point means a long walk back.

Masked Stranger — First Story Boss

The main antagonist of Grounded 2 and your first real boss test. The Masked Stranger uses fast melee combos and repositions constantly.

Strategy

  • Bring a shield. Perfect blocks interrupt the combo string and open counterattack windows
  • Stay mid-range — the lunge attack has a tell: a brief crouch before the dash. Dodge sideways, not backward
  • Use PEEP.R first to identify weak points and resistances before committing
  • Recommended gear: Acorn or Ladybug armor, tier-2 melee weapon, see armor guide

O.R.C. Broodmother & Masked Fighter — Duo Fight

The second story boss is a two-enemy encounter: a broodmother that summons spiderlings while the Masked Fighter presses you with melee. Managing both at once is the challenge.

Strategy

  • Kill the Fighter first. The Broodmother replaces spiderlings endlessly; the Fighter doesn't respawn. Clearing him turns the fight into a manageable single-target duel
  • Bring AoE or a buggy mount — a Red Soldier Ant Buggy helps tank spiderling pressure
  • Use the arena terrain — pillars block the Broodmother's web shots; break line of sight to force her to reposition
  • Recommended gear: tier-2/3 gear, Roach armor for status resistance against venom
Co-op tip
In multiplayer, assign one player to spiderling duty while the rest focus the boss. Spiderlings ignored for too long will overwhelm the arena.

AXL — Optional Tarantula Boss

AXL is an optional tarantula boss with a distinctive weakness: a vulnerable scar on its abdomen. This fight unlocks the Tarantula armor track — one of the best armor lines in the game.

Strategy

  • Attack the abdomen scar for bonus damage. AXL exposes it after certain lunging attacks — that's your punish window
  • Watch the rear kick. Standing behind AXL is safe only during the punish window; it kicks otherwise
  • Bring a repairable shield and full Stamina mutations — this is a long fight
  • Note: weapons thrown at AXL no longer fall through terrain (fixed in Toxic Tangle), so throwing weapons are viable

King Dozer — The Deadliest Creature in the Park

A horned lizard boss and arguably the hardest encounter in Grounded 2. King Dozer hits like a truck, and its scales craft top-tier King Dozer gear.

Strategy

  • Max out before attempting: tier-3 weapons, upgraded armor, and Milk Molar upgrades spent on health and stamina (see our Milk Molars guide)
  • The charge is the fight. King Dozer telegraphs charges with a head-lower animation. Dodge perpendicular — never backward
  • Bait, punish, retreat. Chip damage after charges; don't get greedy with combo strings
  • Bee armor + charged bow shots let you safely slow and kite — the safest solo strategy
  • Buggy mounts help but aren't immune — Dozer can knock you off; dismount when it charges

Snake Colossus — Toxic Tangle Encounter

Added in the Toxic Tangle update, the Snake Colossus guards the Community Garden's deeper paths. Obsidian teases it as one of the park's most dangerous encounters — treat it as a late-game check.

  • Bring Sizzle/hazard protection — the Garden's routes apply status effects
  • Study the attack pattern for two or three attempts before committing consumables
  • Check our Toxic Tangle update guide for the full Garden breakdown

Boss Prep Checklist

  1. PEEP.R scan the boss on first encounter — learn weaknesses before the real attempt
  2. Full armor set bonus active (check equipment panel)
  3. Weapon matches the boss's weakness (see weapon tier list)
  4. Cooked food + full canteen + repair materials
  5. Lean-To respawn set near the arena
  6. Best mutations equipped for your build

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bosses are in Grounded 2?

Currently four major bosses (Masked Stranger, O.R.C. Broodmother & Masked Fighter, AXL, King Dozer) plus the Snake Colossus encounter from the Toxic Tangle update. More bosses are planned in the 2026 roadmap — including new locations like the Black Anthill.

What is the hardest boss in Grounded 2?

King Dozer is widely considered the deadliest creature in the park. AXL is close behind for solo players without a maxed blocking build.

Can you cheese bosses with buggies?

Partially. Buggy mounts add mobility and extra damage, but bosses have anti-mount mechanics — you can be knocked off, and some attacks ignore the mount entirely.
