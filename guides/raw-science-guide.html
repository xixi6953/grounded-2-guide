Grounded 2 Raw Science: How to Farm It Fast

Updated August 22, 2026 - Into the Abyss update

Raw Science (RS) is Grounded 2's special currency. It doesn't buy gear directly — it unlocks recipes, buys upgrades, and pays for essential progression items like the Diving Suit recipes (1,500 RS each). If you've stared at a Science Shop wondering how anyone affords it, this guide is for you.

What Raw Science Actually Is

Raw Science is the glowing purple currency you earn from exploring and interacting with the world. It appears as:

  • RS nodes — small glowing purple orbs scattered around the yard, often near landmarks, in caves, and around creature nests. Interact to collect.
  • Quest/challenge rewards — completing challenges and milestones pays RS.
  • Turning in items — certain discs and collectibles (like Aquatic Turbo Discs) award a lump sum when turned in at Ranger Stations.
  • Scanning discoveries — scanning new creatures and items at Resource Analyzers yields RS alongside recipe unlocks.

Where RS Spends: The Science Shop

Every Ranger Station has a Science Shop. Major purchases include:

Golden rule
Grab all three Aquatic Turbo Discs before spending big at the Science Shop — then unlock the three Diving Suit recipes in one go. Don't drain your RS on low-priority unlocks first.

Farming Methods by Game Stage

Early Game: Node Runs + Scanning

  • Scan everything new. Every first-time scan of a creature, plant, or item at a Resource Analyzer pays RS. Early game, you're discovering constantly — this is your income.
  • Node sweeps: run a loop through known areas (near your base, along the garden wall, around landmarks) collecting the glowing RS orbs. Nodes respawn over time, so repeat the loop daily.
  • Milestones: check your challenge list — simple tasks (kill X bugs, build Y structures) often reward RS.

Mid Game: Disc Turn-Ins + Buggy Exploration

  • Collectible discs scattered around the map pay lump sums at Ranger Stations. With a Buggy mount, you can reach discs in dangerous or remote zones quickly.
  • Explore new biomes: each new area is dense with RS nodes and scanning opportunities — one trip into a new zone can pay hundreds.
  • The picnic-table lock case: the fixed combination case on the picnic table holds exactly 500 Raw Science plus a Milk Molar. Free money if you know the code.

Late Game: Abyss Prep

  • Before the Abyss push, farm challenges and repeat node loops while gathering materials.
  • Budget 4,500 RS minimum for the three Diving Suit recipes, plus extra for utility unlocks you'll want inside the Abyss.

Fast Farming Route Tips

  1. Pick a loop, not a spot. RS nodes respawn — a 10-minute circuit past 15–20 node locations beats camping one area.
  2. Pair farming with other tasks. Collect nodes on the way to material runs — never travel empty.
  3. Scan aggressively. Every new biome, weapon, and creature is RS waiting in the Analyzer. Keep a mental "unscanned" list.
  4. Do challenges in batches. Many challenges complete naturally — claim their rewards in the menu so the RS actually lands in your wallet.
  5. Mount up. A fast Buggy turns a 20-minute node loop into 8 minutes. Speed is money.

FAQ

Do RS nodes respawn?

Yes — nodes return over time, making daily loop routes a reliable income source.

What's the biggest single RS source?

Turning in discs and special collectibles at Ranger Stations gives the largest lump sums, plus challenge completion rewards.

I need 4,500 RS for the Diving Suit but I'm broke. Fastest fix?

Do three things: clear every unclaimed challenge reward, sweep a full node loop on a fast mount, and scan your backlog of unscanned items. Most players are sitting on 1,000+ RS of unclaimed rewards without realizing it.
