Grounded 2 Milk Molars: Locations & Upgrade Priority

Updated August 15, 2026 - Covers Into the Abyss & Toxic Tangle content

Milk Molars are Grounded 2's permanent upgrade currency. Unlike gear — which you'll replace dozens of times — Milk Molar upgrades stick with your character forever: extra health, more stamina, bigger carry weight, better healing. If you feel underpowered, the answer is usually Milk Molars, not a new weapon.

The one rule of Milk Molars
Always carry your Omni-Hammer when hunting Molars. Busting a Milk Molar requires the Omni-Hammer form of the Omni-Tool — arriving without it means walking back empty-handed.

How Milk Molars Work

  1. Find them in the world — Milk Molars are tucked into landmarks, labs, anthills, ranger outposts, and hidden paths throughout Brookhollow Park
  2. Bust them with the Omni-Hammer — approach and smash to collect
  3. Spend them at upgrade terminals — convert Molars into permanent character upgrades

There are two tiers:

ItemWhat It UpgradesRarity
Milk MolarCore stats — health, stamina, carry weight, healing efficiencyScattered everywhere, easy to miss
Mega Milk MolarBigger, build-defining upgrade stepsHidden in dangerous or hard-to-reach spots

Where to Find Milk Molars

Molars spawn consistently in these location types across every zone:

  • Anthills — both red and black anthill tunnels; watch for soldier patrols
  • Labs — interior rooms and behind gated mechanisms (bring the Omni-Wrench)
  • Ranger Outposts — usually 1-2 per outpost; often near Resource Analyzers
  • New Garden landmarks — the Toxic Tangle Community Garden added vertical climbing routes with Molars along hidden paths
  • Under and inside large landmarks — juice boxes, toys, furniture; check underneath and inside
  • Burrow and cave dead-ends — the game rewards thorough exploration
Mega Molar danger check
Mega Milk Molars are deliberately placed near threats — spider dens, scorpion burrows, or hazard zones. Prepare hazard gear before climbing for the obvious shiny one.

Upgrade Priority: What to Buy First

Molar upgrades are permanent, so order matters less than weapons — but efficiency still counts. Recommended order for most players:

  1. Health upgrades — survive one extra hit changes every fight, especially boss encounters
  2. Stamina upgrades — more blocking, more dodging, more charged attacks
  3. Carry weight — fewer trips back to base; pairs with the Hauling Hero mutation from our mutations guide
  4. Healing efficiency — each healing item goes further
  5. Utility upgrades — buy these once your core stats feel comfortable

Milk Molar Hunting Route Strategy

  1. Upgrade your Omni-Tool first — the Omni-Hammer and Omni-Wrench functions gate many Molar locations (see the beginner guide progression path)
  2. Sweep one zone per trip — pick a zone (e.g., an anthill or the Garden), clear every landmark, then move on
  3. Mark what you can't reach — gated Molars are a reason to return once your tools upgrade
  4. Go at night for the risky ones — know your predator activity; some zones are calmer after dark, others worse

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get all Milk Molar upgrades?

With thorough exploration, yes — the game provides more Molars than needed to max every track, so don't stress about perfect efficiency. Spend freely on stats you actually use.

Why can't I bust this Milk Molar?

You need the Omni-Hammer form of the Omni-Tool. If the bust prompt doesn't appear, your Omni-Tool isn't upgraded yet — progress the story and Raw Science upgrades.

Are Milk Molars shared in co-op?

Collected Molars count per player — coordinate with teammates so everyone gets the bust, or take turns farming zones.
