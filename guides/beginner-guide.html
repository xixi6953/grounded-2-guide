Grounded 2 Beginner Guide: Your First 10 Hours

Updated August 15, 2026 - Current as of Into the Abyss update (v0.3)

Grounded 2 launched on PS5 on August 11, 2026 alongside the Into the Abyss update, bringing thousands of new players into Brookhollow Park. If you're just starting out, this guide walks you through your first 10 hours, from grabbing the Omni-Tool to getting your first buggy mount.

Do I need to play Grounded 1 first?
No. Grounded 2 is a standalone experience with a new story set in Brookhollow Park. The Omni-Tool, buggy mounts, and archetype system are all new mechanics. No prior knowledge required.

Hour 0-1: Follow the Story, Get the Omni-Tool

The biggest mistake new players make is wandering off to pick flowers instead of following the story quests. Don't. The opening quests hand you the Omni-Tool, which combines the axe, shovel, hammer, and wrench into a single tool. Without it, you can't harvest most materials.

Follow the quest markers until the Omni-Tool is in your hands. While you're at it, craft these basics:

  • Pebblet Spear - your first weapon for anything that bites
  • Bow - ranged option once you can spare the parts
  • Warhammerlet - best early stun weapon
  • Red Ant Armor - cheap early defense set

Hour 1-3: Build Your First Base

Where to build

Pick a spot with flat ground, near clean water, and away from spider patrols. Good starter locations include open grass clearings and areas under landmarks like the juice box. You need a spot you can find again in the dark.

What to build first

  1. Lean-To - rest point and respawn anchor. Interact with it to set it as your respawn (many players forget this step).
  2. Workbench - required for crafting most gear
  3. Storage Chests - you'll come back from every trip with full pockets; without chests you'll be throwing away good materials
  4. Roasting Spit - cook bug parts before eating them raw
  5. Canteen - carry water between ponds and dew drops (recipe unlocks after scanning Grub Hide)
Don't build near webs
If you see webs, ant trails, or scorpion burrows near your build site, move. Bugs will investigate your base, and early-game walls won't stop them.

Hour 3-5: The Scanning Loop

Raw Science (RS) is the currency that unlocks recipes. The fastest way to earn it is to scan every new thing you find at a Resource Analyzer located at Ranger Outposts.

The scanning loop works like this:

  1. Go out and gather unfamiliar resources
  2. Haul them to the nearest Resource Analyzer
  3. Feed them in to unlock crafting recipes and earn RS
  4. Spend RS on Omni-Tool upgrades and new recipes

This is the engine that drives your progression. Skipping it means hitting walls where you can't craft essential items.

Hour 5-7: Upgrade the Omni-Tool, Get a Buggy

Omni-Tool upgrades

Your starting Omni-Tool only has the axe function. You need to unlock the shovel and hammer upgrades as soon as possible, because higher-tier resources require specific tool types to harvest. A Tier 2 chopping function is the gate to most mid-game gear.

Upgrades cost Raw Science and materials like roach chunks and scorpion stingers. The story quests guide you toward these naturally.

Getting your first buggy

This is where Grounded 2 transforms. After finding a Red Soldier Ant Egg and building a Hatchery, you can hatch a Red Soldier Ant Buggy. Benefits:

  • Speed - the park is massive; walking takes forever
  • Storage - each buggy has its own inventory, doubling carry capacity
  • Combat - your buggy fights alongside you and can recruit other ants

The story quests lead you toward this. Look for ant eggs in ant hills. The Hatchery blueprint unlocks through the quest line.

Hour 7-10: Archetypes & Combat

Choose your archetype

Grounded 2's class system is tied to your gear, not a permanent choice. Switch freely by changing armor and weapons:

ArchetypePlaystyleKey Gear
WarriorTank, heavy weapons, blockingLadybug armor, sword & shield
RangerRanged, bow-focusedOrb Weaver set, bow
MageElemental, spellcastingCandy staves, Butterfly armor
RogueFast, dual daggers, venomGap-closing attacks

If you're struggling with combat, start with Warrior using a shield. Perfect blocks trigger parry stuns that create counterattack openings.

Combat basics

  • Use PEEP.R - scan every new creature before fighting. It reveals weaknesses, resistances, and vulnerable points.
  • Learn to block - perfect blocking is the most important combat skill. It stuns the attacker and opens a counterattack window.
  • Match damage types - slashing, stabbing, and blunt damage work differently against different bugs. PEEP.R tells you which to use.
  • Don't fight spiders or scorpions early - these are mid-to-late game enemies. Stick to mites and larvae as training dummies.

What's Next?

After your first 10 hours, you should have a functional base, an upgraded Omni-Tool, a buggy mount, and basic combat competence. From here:

  • Follow the main story through Act 1 (roughly 18 hours of story content)
  • Explore the new Garden biome from The Toxic Tangle update
  • Check our weapon tier list to plan your crafting priorities
  • Consult the creature bestiary before tackling tougher enemies
