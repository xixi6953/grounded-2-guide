The Toxic Tangle Update: Everything New in Grounded 2

Updated August 15, 2026 - Grounded 2 v0.3 (January 27, 2026 patch)

The Toxic Tangle is the first major content update for Grounded 2, expanding Brookhollow Park with a new Garden biome, a rideable Ladybug Buggy, 8 new creatures, 6 new armor sets, 12+ new weapons, a mutation system overhaul, and new building kits. This guide covers everything that changed.

Also: Into the Abyss (Aug 11)
Alongside the PS5 launch on August 11, the Into the Abyss update cracked open the pond area across all platforms. This guide focuses on The Toxic Tangle content, but both updates are live simultaneously.

New Biome: The Garden

A massive new area with overgrown vines choking a greenhouse. Key features:

  • Vertical exploration - climbing paths, hidden crawlspaces, and "how do I get up there?" structures
  • Pumpkin Patch - harvestable pumpkins for building and food
  • Carrot Jungle - dig carrots, fight Potato Beetles, find Keylimes
  • Garden MIX.R - new wave challenge station
  • Garden O.R.C. - new O.R.C. wave challenges for high-level players
  • Tang mechanics - a corruption spreading through the soil, affecting creatures and blocking access to some areas
Tang-covered creatures
Some creatures in the Garden are covered in Tang, making them more aggressive. The Ladybug Buggy's water cannon is the primary way to rinse away Tang buildup and calm or weaken these enemies.

New Mount: Ladybug Buggy

One of Grounded's most iconic bugs is now rideable. The Ladybug Buggy is a heavy-duty, tank-style mount:

  • Water Cannon - blast airborne pests out of the sky, rinse Tang buildup, or soak enemies
  • Ram Charge - powerful headbutt that deals heavy damage and stuns
  • HP Regeneration - slowly heals over time without consumables

Egg source: Ladybug Nest in the Garden biome. See our full buggy mounts guide for hatchery recipe and upgrade paths.

New Trinkets

  • Pincer Pincher Trinket - increases grab range and interaction speed
  • Fabulous Femur Trinket - chance to trigger a crit buff (nerfed: trigger 20% to 7%, crit buff 50% to 25%, duration 5s to 3s)

Mutation System Overhaul

Mutations are now split into two categories:

  • Active mutations - work the same as before, manually activated
  • Passive mutations - apply automatically based on your weapon loadout. Less menu juggling, more fighting.

New and revamped mutations:

  • Hauling Hero (returning) - carry weight boost. Now only works on foot, not while riding buggies.
  • Bardic Inspiration (revamped) - boosts nearby ally effects
  • Sour Sensation (revamped) - enhances Sour-element weapon damage
  • Whittle Wizard (revamped) - improves crafting efficiency

New Building Kits

  • Pumpkin Set - seasonal building pieces from harvested pumpkins
  • Mushroom Kit (reskin) - refreshed visual style for mushroom building pieces
  • Scale Roof Set - new roofing option for sloped structures
  • Acorn Stairs Kit (update) - improved snapping and parity
  • Floor Hatches - community-requested. Vertical base access between floors.
  • Thatch Awning Kit - decorative roofing for outdoor structures
  • Ponic Patch - planting plot for farming
  • Ladybug Nest - structure for housing Ladybug Buggy eggs
Founder's Pack additions
The Toxic Tangle also expanded Founder's Pack content: High Roller's Dice Bag, Champion's Brazier, Champion's Banner, Adventurer's Table, Throne of P'wn.

Quality of Life Improvements

  • Coordinates now visible in the map menu
  • Upgraded weapons keep their hotpouch assignment
  • Jerky Rack and Roasting Spit interfaces updated, now allow proximity chests
  • Unique weapons can now be crafted via the workbench
  • Glowsticks glow brighter and longer
  • Drawn arrows can be cancelled
  • Bestiary and item ordering made consistent
  • Pebblet path and curved path cost reduced to 3 pebblets
  • More space between players and buggies for fluid movement

Bug Fixes (Highlights)

  • Seasonal decorations no longer crash the game if left past their holiday
  • Fixed jump speed/distance on player-built structures
  • Milkweed pods near Snackbar no longer fall through terrain
  • Dandelion Tufts can no longer trigger on surface edges
  • Mosquitoes no longer fall asleep mid-air
  • Weapons thrown at AXL no longer fall through terrain
  • Camera lens can now be repositioned in freecam mode
  • Early-game resources repositioned for easier access

Returning Content

Several creatures, armors, and weapons from earlier Grounded content returned in this update:

  • Creatures: Firefly, Black Soldier Ant, Black Worker Ant
  • Armor: Firefly Head Lamp, Black Ant set (Legplates, Arm Guards, Helmet, Shield)
  • Weapons: Black Ant Sword, Rusty Spear
  • Station: Glue Masher (updated with new recipes)
  • Holiday items: Valentine's Chocolate Smoochies, Fortune, Cupid's Arrow, and new Great Cupid's Arrow

What's Next?

Obsidian has confirmed continued support through Early Access. With the PS5 launch and Into the Abyss update bringing a wave of new players, expect more content drops throughout 2026. The Garden storyline with The Masked Stranger and the Snake Colossus appears to be setting up for a larger narrative arc.
