Grounded 2 Base Building: Best Locations & Complete Tips

Updated August 15, 2026 - Covers Into the Abyss & Toxic Tangle content

Your base is home, storage, crafting hub, and respawn point all in one. In Grounded 2 it's also under real threat — the park's bugs will investigate, raid, and chew through walls that were fine yesterday. This guide covers where to build, what to build, and how to defend it.

Best Base Locations

Early Game: Starter Spots

  • Open grass clearings near water — flat ground, dew drops for thirst, easy to find
  • Under raised landmarks (juice box, toys) — natural roof, bugs path around them
  • Near the starting Ranger Outpost — quick access to the Resource Analyzer for the scanning loop

Elevation matters
Raised bases (on rocks, roots, or built platforms) are dramatically safer than ground-level ones. Most raiding bugs struggle to reach elevated floors, and you can funnel anything that climbs into door chokepoints.

Mid-Late Game: Forward Bases

Brookhollow Park is huge — one mega-base means long supply runs. Experienced players run a main base plus small forward camps: a Lean-To, a chest, and a roasting spit near each new zone. The Toxic Tangle's Community Garden and its vertical routes make a forward camp almost mandatory.

Building Set Progression

Building materials tier up alongside your tools:

  1. Grass & Clover — starter walls and floors; cheap, weak, fine for week one
  2. Acorn & Stem-based sets — durable mid-game walls that hold against most raids
  3. Mushroom, Pumpkin, Acorn, and Scale sets — added in Toxic Tangle; stronger and better looking
  4. Specialized kits — functional buildings that unlock new systems (see below)

Essential Base Buildings (Build Order)

  1. Lean-To — sleep, respawn anchor. Interact to SET it as respawn (commonly missed — see the beginner guide)
  2. Workbench — gates most crafting recipes
  3. Storage Chests — full pockets on every trip; chests turn loot into an arsenal
  4. Roasting Spit & Jerky Rack — cooked food and preserved travel snacks (interfaces now support proximity chests)
  5. Resource Analyzer link — build near one or plan your scanning runs
  6. Hatchery — required for buggy mounts
  7. Smithing Station — armor and weapon upgrades
  8. Glue Masher — updated with new recipes; key crafting station
  9. Defensive perimeter — walls, floor hatches, chokepoints

Base Defense Against Raids

  • Elevate the core. Put your chests and stations on a raised floor with one stairs entrance
  • Chokepoint everything. Bugs that must path through one door die in one door — meet them with a tier-S weapon
  • Floor Hatches seal vertical entrances without walling yourself in
  • Repair kits in stock — keep repair ingredients in a chest next to the wall, not back in storage
  • Don't over-aggro. Bases near aggressive nests get raided more; check the bestiary for territory info before settling
Terrain patch warning
any fix that changes the map can shift geometry — and sometimes your base with it. After big updates, screenshot your base location and check coordinates (now shown in the map menu after Toxic Tangle).

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best base location in Grounded 2?

There's no single best spot — pick flat, elevated ground near water, away from webs and ant trails. A main base near the starting area plus forward camps in each new zone is the strongest overall setup.

Can bugs destroy walls?

Yes. Raids chew through grass walls quickly and tougher sets slowly. Elevation and chokepoints matter more than wall material — bugs that can't reach your floor can't break it.

What should I build first?

Lean-To (set respawn!), Workbench, and Storage Chests — in that order. Everything else can wait.
